Lesson 11.1

Read a color from the glass picture

11.1
S = (P_x + d, P_y)
C = \operatorname{sample}(S)
POutput pixel. Its center has horizontal position P_x and vertical position P_y, in points from the view’s top-left corner. dHorizontal read distance in points. Positive goes right. Negative goes left. SSource point in the glass picture. CPremultiplied red, green, blue, and alpha read at S and returned for P.
Worked read Glass picture with an asymmetric amber stripe and menu rows
InputsP = (\kn{174}, \kn{58})\,\mathrm{pt},\quad d = \kn{10}\,\mathrm{pt}
Source x\kn{174} + \kn{10} = 184\,\mathrm{pt}
Source y\kn{58} + \kn{0} = 58\,\mathrm{pt}
OutputReturn the color at (184, 58) pt for P at (174, 58) pt.

maxSampleOffset is 24 pt per axis here, from the planned maximum in-flight read. The test reads 10 pt.
