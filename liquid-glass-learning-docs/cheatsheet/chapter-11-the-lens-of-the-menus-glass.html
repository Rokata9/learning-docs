Lessons 11.1–11.2

Read a color from the glass picture

11.1
S = (P_x + d, P_y)
C = \operatorname{sample}(S)
POutput pixel. Its center has horizontal position P_x and vertical position P_y, in points from the view’s top-left corner. dHorizontal read distance in points. Positive goes right. Negative goes left. SSource point in the glass picture. CPremultiplied red, green, blue, and alpha read at S and returned for P.
Worked read Glass picture with an asymmetric amber stripe and menu rows
InputsP = (\kn{174}, \kn{58})\,\mathrm{pt},\quad d = \kn{10}\,\mathrm{pt}
Source x\kn{174} + \kn{10} = 184\,\mathrm{pt}
Source y\kn{58} + \kn{0} = 58\,\mathrm{pt}
OutputReturn the color at (184, 58) pt for P at (174, 58) pt.

maxSampleOffset is 24 pt per axis here, from the planned maximum in-flight read. The test reads 10 pt.

Read farther inside along the rim

11.2
L = w - \min(d,w),\quad h = \sqrt{w^2 - L^2}
p = m \times \left(1 - \frac{h}{w}\right),\quad S = P - U \times p
POutput pixel’s position in screen points. dDistance from the outline Q to P, in points inside the glass. wWidth of the rim in points. It is also the quarter circle’s radius and the full height. LHorizontal leg of the side-view triangle. It runs from P to the circle’s center K while P is under the rim. hHeight from P to the curve, in points. mLargest read distance, at the outline, in points. pRead distance at P, in points. UOutward direction from outwardAt, scaled to length 1. SSource position in the glass picture. Return the color from S for P.
Worked read Resting upper-left corner, then the same depth in flight
Resting inputsP = (\kn{126.06}, \kn{660.28})\,\mathrm{pt},\quad d = \kn{5.6}\,\mathrm{pt},\quad w = \kn{14}\,\mathrm{pt},\quad m = \kn{6}\,\mathrm{pt}
Horizontal legL = \kn{14} - \kn{5.6} = 8.4\,\mathrm{pt}
Height above P \kn{14}^2 = 196\,\mathrm{pt}^2
8.4^2 = 70.56\,\mathrm{pt}^2
h^2 = 196 - 70.56 = 125.44\,\mathrm{pt}^2
h = \sqrt{125.44} = 11.2\,\mathrm{pt}
Push 11.2 \div \kn{14} = 0.8 of the height climbed
\kn{1} - 0.8 = 0.2 of the height left
p = \kn{6} \times 0.2 = 1.2\,\mathrm{pt}
Source at rest U \approx (\kn{-0.60}, \kn{-0.80})
\kn{-0.60} \times 1.2 = -0.72\,\mathrm{pt} outward along x
\kn{-0.80} \times 1.2 = -0.96\,\mathrm{pt} outward along y
S_x = \kn{126.06} - (-0.72) = 126.78\,\mathrm{pt}
S_y = \kn{660.28} - (-0.96) = 661.24\,\mathrm{pt}
Resting endpoints At the outline, h = \kn{0}\,\mathrm{pt} and the read uses the full \kn{6}\,\mathrm{pt}.
On the flat top, h = \kn{14}\,\mathrm{pt} and the read is 0\,\mathrm{pt}.
In flight Same depth d = \kn{5.6}\,\mathrm{pt} with w = \kn{28}\,\mathrm{pt},\quad m = \kn{24}\,\mathrm{pt}
L = \kn{28} - \kn{5.6} = 22.4\,\mathrm{pt}
\kn{28}^2 = 784\,\mathrm{pt}^2
22.4^2 = 501.76\,\mathrm{pt}^2
h^2 = 784 - 501.76 = 282.24\,\mathrm{pt}^2
h = \sqrt{282.24} = 16.8\,\mathrm{pt}
16.8 \div \kn{28} = 0.6 of the height climbed
\kn{1} - 0.6 = 0.4 of the height left
p = \kn{24} \times 0.4 = 9.6\,\mathrm{pt}

The course fits these two rim settings to the visible bending in the Safari opening. The quarter circle and the height rule are model choices.
