Lesson 9.13

The four fringe colors: yellow, red, cyan, blue

9.13
\begin{aligned} \color{#dc2626}{\mathrm{readRed}} &= \operatorname{clamp}(\mathrm{uv} - \mathrm{disp} \times (1 - \mathrm{spread}),\; 0,\; 1) \\ \color{#16a34a}{\mathrm{readGreen}} &= \operatorname{clamp}(\mathrm{uv} - \mathrm{disp},\; 0,\; 1) \\ \color{#2563eb}{\mathrm{readBlue}} &= \operatorname{clamp}(\mathrm{uv} - \mathrm{disp} \times (1 + \mathrm{spread}),\; 0,\; 1) \\ \mathrm{color} &= \mathrm{half4}(\color{#dc2626}{\mathrm{colRed.r}},\; \color{#16a34a}{\mathrm{colGreen.g}},\; \color{#2563eb}{\mathrm{colBlue.b}},\; 1) \\ \text{strip width} &= \mathrm{spread} \times \mathrm{push} \end{aligned}
\mathrm{uv}the pixel, in UV: 0 … 1 across the picture, y grows downward. \mathrm{disp}the push arrow of Lesson 9.11, dir × push. dir points from C to the pixel, so uv − disp moves toward C. \mathrm{push}the arrow's length, dispLen × falloff: 0.10 up to 0.15 from C, fading to 0 at the orange ring 0.35 from C. \mathrm{spread}0.10: red's push is 10% shorter than green's, blue's 10% longer. \color{#dc2626}{\mathrm{readRed}},\; \color{#16a34a}{\mathrm{readGreen}},\; \color{#2563eb}{\mathrm{readBlue}}the three read points of one pixel, in this order along the pink arrow, spread × push apart. \color{#dc2626}{\mathrm{colRed.r}}the red member of the wallpaper color at readRed (Lesson 8.7); colGreen.g and colBlue.b in the same way. \text{strip width}how wide one colored strip is in the output, in UV.

When each color appears

Walk along the pink arrow, from readRed to readBlue. A channel is 1 where its read point is on white, 0 where it is on black.

  • White → black gives yellow, then red. Black → white gives cyan, then blue.
  • Two channels on (yellow, cyan): next to white. One channel on (red, blue): next to black.
  • No edge between readRed and readBlue: all three read one color, so no fringe (the lesson's Exercise 1). No push: no fringe.
  • Swap the red and blue pushes, and white → black gives cyan, then blue (the lesson's Exercise 2).

Where the strips are.
