Every formula of the course so far, one page per topic. Each card names the lessons it comes from. Click a lesson chip to open that lesson.

An orange chip such as DensityExplorer.swift opens the Swift file of the iOS lab that shows the formula at work. These links work on the Mac that has the lab at Code/LiquidGlassLab-iOS, next to this repository.

The Sections button on the left edge of every page lists all sections.
