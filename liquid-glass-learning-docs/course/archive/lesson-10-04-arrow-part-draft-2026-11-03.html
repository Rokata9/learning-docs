Lesson 10.4 — An arrow's part is a foot on an axis

Draw one part of N as a place on a line. Light theme · desktop · works from file://.

Lesson 10.3 gave every pixel an arrow, N, with three parts. So far those parts are numbers. This lesson finds each number as a place in the picture: a line from the tip of N, at a square corner to the x line, meets the x line exactly where N's x part ends. Lesson 10.5 makes the same drawing onto a line in any direction, and that gives the dot product.

Section 3 · exercisesExercises

Read each picture, pick one answer, then open the hidden reason.

Exercise 1 — Where does the line from the tip of N meet the height line?

Why this question. The x line is level, and the height line stands straight up. The picture looks different, so it is worth checking that the drawing still reads a part of N.

What you do. Start the walk with the leg that lies on the height line. Ask which way the other two legs go. Then read how long the first leg is.

Question: where does the line from the tip of N meet the height line?

Show the answer
  • The height leg goes first. It ends at Gh, 0.864 above S.
  • The x leg and the y leg go 0 up. So both stay at 0.864 above S, on a level wall.
  • The height line stands on that wall like a pole on a floor.
  • So the gray line from the tip of N meets the height line at a square corner, at Gh.
Exercise 2 — Where does the line from the tip of N at K₂ meet the x line?

Why this question. At K the y leg went toward the bottom of the picture. At K₂ it goes the opposite way. It is easy to think that this moves the foot on the x line.

What you do. N at K₂ is (0.302, −0.402, 0.864). Find the leg that lies on the x line. Ask how far the other two legs go to the right.

Question: where does the line from the tip of N at K₂ meet the x line?

Show the answer
  • The x leg ends at Gx, 0.302 to the right of S₂.
  • The y leg now goes toward the top of the picture. It still stays 0.302 to the right of S₂.
  • The height leg stays there too.
  • So the tip of N is on the same wall, and the gray line meets the x line at Gx.
