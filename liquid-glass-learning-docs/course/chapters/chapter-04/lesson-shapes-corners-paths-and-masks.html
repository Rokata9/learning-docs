Chapter 4

Shapes, corners, paths, and masks

Chapter 3 explained how colors combine at one drawing position. Chapter 4 explains the geometry that decides which positions belong to a glass control. Excerpts use SwiftUI Path, Canvas, and Swift explorers in LiquidGlassLab-iOS.

3 · NEW VOCABULARY

Words this chapter will define before use

Bounds

Four edges that enclose a rectangular area.

Radius

One distance from a circle center to its boundary.

Path

An ordered set of drawing instructions that builds a shape boundary.

Clip

A temporary region that restricts where selected draw calls may write.

Mask

A separate value field that controls where another drawing or effect is visible.
