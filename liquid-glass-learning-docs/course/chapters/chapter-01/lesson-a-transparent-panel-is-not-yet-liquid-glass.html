Chapter 1 · Eight small lessons

A transparent panel is not yet Liquid Glass

Chapter 1 builds a deliberately incomplete result. The point is diagnosis. You will learn to name the operations that are present and the operations that are still missing.

Chapter checkpoint

You can explain why a translucent rounded panel is not blur and is not refraction. You can point to the visible evidence.

Deliberately postponed

Chapter 1 does not implement real backdrop blur, displaced sampling, refraction, physical edge lighting, 3D perspective, or liquid shape transitions. Those topics need coordinate systems, image sampling, shaders, and motion math. The course teaches those dependencies before using them.
