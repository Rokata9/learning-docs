Four misunderstandings to avoid

“The raw totals must be 0 to 15.”No. The totals can be large. Only their remainders are restricted to 0 through 15.
“The outputs must appear in numerical order.”No. They appear in a shuffled order such as 12, 3, 10, 1, …, but every value still appears once.
“The row may change while the column changes.”Not in this claim. The row contribution must be the same shared constant for all 16 columns.
“One example is the proof.”An example helps us see the mechanism. The no-collision gap argument is what proves it for every starting column and fixed row.
