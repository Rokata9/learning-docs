Why the earlier image-edge topic was postponed

An image-edge artifact cannot be explained until the course has introduced the operation that calculates new image values, such as antialiasing or image sampling. Those operations come later. Lesson 3.5 teaches the two RGBA representations through the complete translucent-source-over-translucent-destination calculation from Lesson 3.3. Lesson 3.6 now continues with one opaque visible color and does not introduce image sampling. Its rejected first draft also tried to reveal the finished luminance pipeline before defining its terms. The replacement begins with a previously learned Color object and teaches one conversion at a time.
