Lesson 10.10 — Half a turn sends T to the opposite point

Light theme · desktop · works from file://.

Spin T half a turn, 180°. T lands on the same line through Gx, on the other side. Lesson 10.11 uses this.

Uses

  • Lesson 10.9: the teal line GxT spins, and T goes round T's circle (|GxT| = 0.953). At about 65°, T is at the tip of N.
  • Lesson 10.3: a quarter turn (90°) takes (1, slope) to (−slope, 1).
  • Lesson 10.7: the wall seen straight on draws y (→) and height (↑) at their real lengths.

Section 3 · exercisesExercises

Exercise 1 — T is on line a at a spin of 40°. At which other spin is T on line a?

Question: T is on line a at a spin of 40°. At which other spin is T on line a?

Show the answer
  • At 40°, T is at (0.730, 0.613) from Gx.
  • Half a turn: (−0.730, −0.613). That is on line a, on the other side of Gx.
  • So the other spin is 40° + 180° = 220°. The gray dots, with one minus only, are off line a.
Exercise 2 — T is at the tip of N. T spins half a turn more. Where is T now, counted from Gx?

Question: T is at the tip of N. T spins half a turn more. Where is T now, counted from Gx?

Show the answer
  • A quarter turn: (a, b) → (−b, a). Twice, from the tip of N:
    • first: (0.402, 0.864) → (−0.864, 0.402)
    • second: (−0.864, 0.402) → (−0.402, −0.864)
  • So T is at (0, −0.402, −0.864) from Gx: the opposite point.
