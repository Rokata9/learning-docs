Lesson 10.4 — The rest of the walk stays on a wall

After the x leg, the walk to the tip of N goes 0 to the right. Light theme · desktop · works from file://.

Lesson 10.3 gave every pixel an arrow, N, with an x part, a y part and a height part. Walk from the start of N to its tip in three straight legs, one per part: after the x leg, the other two legs go 0 to the right, so the rest of the walk stays on one wall. Lesson 10.16 uses this wall to show that a line from the tip of N, at a square corner to the x line (the level line through S along x), meets it where the x leg ends; Lesson 10.18 draws such a line toward any direction, and where it meets says how far N goes that way.

New here
  • A leg of the walk: one straight piece of the walk from S to the tip of N. Step 1.
  • The x line and its ruler. Step 2.
  • The one idea: the y leg and the height leg go 0 to the right (Steps 3 and 4). So after the x leg, the rest of the walk stays on the wall: all points 0.302 to the right of S. Step 5.

Exercise 1 — How far to the right of S₂ is the tip of N at K₂?

Why this question. At K the y leg went toward the bottom of the picture. At K₂ it goes the opposite way. Does that change how far to the right the walk ends?

What you do. Follow the three legs in the picture. For each leg, ask how far it goes to the right. Then add.

Question: how far to the right of S₂ is the tip of N at K₂?

Show the answer
  • The x leg ends at Gx₂, 0.302 to the right of S₂.
  • The y leg goes toward the top of the picture. It goes 0 to the right: 0.302 + 0 = 0.302.
  • The height leg goes up. It goes 0 to the right: 0.302 + 0 = 0.302.
  • So the tip of N is on the gray wall: all points 0.302 to the right of S₂.
Exercise 2 — Which point is on the wall 0.302 to the right of S?

Why this question. Each point has three numbers. Only one of them decides whether the point is on the wall.

What you do. Read the three numbers of each point in the table. Find the number that says how far to the right of S the point is.

Question: which point is on the wall 0.302 to the right of S?

Show the answer
  • The gray wall: all points 0.302 to the right of S.
  • P₁'s x leg ends at Gx. Its y leg and its height leg go 0 to the right. So P₁ is on the wall.
  • P₂'s x leg goes 0.45 to the right, past the wall. P₃'s goes 0.15, short of it.
  • Their other legs go 0 to the right. So P₂ and P₃ stay off the wall.
