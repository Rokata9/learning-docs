Lesson 10.8 — The spin keeps the length

Spin the teal line around the x line: Gx to T stays 0.953 long. Light theme · desktop · works from file://.

In Lesson 10.6 the spin turned the teal line around the x line and kept it on the wall. In Lesson 10.7 the wall seen straight on drew every line on the wall at its whole length. This lesson uses that drawing to show that the spin keeps the teal line's length: Gx to T stays 0.953 at every spin. Lesson 10.9 uses it to find T's path.

Exercise 1 — The teal line has spun 140°. How far from Gx is T now?

Why this question. In this angled drawing the teal line now looks clearly longer than at its start (the pixel numbers). Has the spin made it longer?

What you do. Remember how the spin moves the teal line in the wall seen straight on (Step 3), and what a turn does to a length (Step 4).

Question: the teal line has spun 140°. How far from Gx is T now?

Show the answer
  • Seen straight on, the spin turns the teal line as one piece around Gx, by 140°.
  • A turn keeps lengths: the teal line is drawn 135.326 px long at 0° and at 140°.
  • 135.326 ÷ 142 = 0.953
  • This drawing shows Gx to T at its whole length. So T is still 0.953 from Gx.
Exercise 2 — After a spin of 120°, how far from Gx is the middle of the teal line?

Why this question. At the start the middle of the teal line is half of 0.953 from Gx. The spin moved it far. Did its distance from Gx change?

What you do. Remember how every point of the teal line moves in the wall seen straight on (Step 3).

Question: after a spin of 120°, how far from Gx is the middle of the teal line?

Show the answer
  • At the start the middle is half of the teal line from Gx:
    • 0.953 ÷ 2 = 0.4765
  • The spin turns the teal line as one straight piece around Gx (Step 3). Its middle turns with it, by the same 120°.
  • A turn keeps lengths. So the middle is drawn 0.4765 × 142 px = 67.663 px from Gx at 0° and at 120°.
  • Gx to the middle has x part 0 (both on the wall), so this drawing shows it at its whole length:
    • 67.663 ÷ 142 = 0.4765
  • So after the spin the middle is still 0.4765 from Gx.
