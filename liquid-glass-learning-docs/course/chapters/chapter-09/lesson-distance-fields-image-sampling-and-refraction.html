3 · VOCABULARY

Words this chapter uses for distance and the read

Signed distance

sd = length(p) − radius. Minus is inside, zero is the boundary, plus is outside. Lesson 9.2 stores orange when sd < 0 and navy otherwise. Default radius is 0.25. Teaching p = (0.30, −0.40) has sd = +0.25.

Soft mask

mask = 1 − smoothstep(−band, band, sd), then mix(navy, orange, mask). Default band is 0.10. At teaching sd = +0.05 the mask is 0.15625 and the stored red is 0.221875.

Rounded rect and capsule

Lesson 9.4 builds q = abs(p) − innerHalf, then sd = sdBox − cornerRadius. Outer half stays (0.30, 0.20). Lesson 9.5 clamps p.x onto the inner segment and measures the circle around that nearest point. Radius equals half-height, 0.20.

Combine

Lesson 9.6 keeps two Lesson 9.2 circles. Union is min. Intersection is max. B minus A is max(sdB, −sdA). A minus B is max(−sdB, sdA). Teaching p = (−0.10, 0) stays sdA = −0.15 and sdB = +0.05.

Metal function call

Lessons 9.7–9.9 call background(coord, resolution) in the same .metal file. That call is not a texture sample. A negative read x is not clamped and not wrapped. A Lesson 9.9 read outside the picture is not clamped. The HTML lab paints that read gray. The Metal function does not.

Displacement

Lesson 9.10: dir = p / length(p), disp = dir × dispLen, readUv = uv − disp. Contract stays W = (0.65, 0.20) and dispLen = 0.10. That read stores navy. Lesson 9.11 multiplies disp by the circle’s inside-amount.

Edge clamp

Lesson 9.12 adds readUv = clamp(readUv, 0.0, 1.0) after the Lesson 9.11 chain. Glass center C = (0.50, 1.10). Teaching W = (0.59, 0.98) reads R = (0.53, 1.06), outside the picture. The clamp lands on (0.53, 1.00) and stores navy. Generated, not complete.

Lens mask

Lesson 9.14 keeps a bent color and adds mask = 1 − smoothstep(−maskBand, maskBand, sd) with maskBand = 0.002. The store is mix(plain, bent, mask). Teaching W = (0.40, 0.24) at time 2 s stores white. The lesson is generated and is not compiled.

5 · CHAPTER CHECKPOINT

Two answers, then one mix

By Lesson 9.14, both answers start from p = uv − center, d = length(p), and sd = d − radius. The bent color is the Lesson 9.13 chain: one channel from each of three clamped reads. The mask is Lesson 9.3’s soft mask with maskBand = 0.002. The store is mix(plain, bent, mask). plain is the wallpaper at the pixel itself. The wallpaper is a checker shifted by speed × time with speed = 0.10 UV per second. Glass center stays (0.40, 0.54), radius 0.25, band 0.10, dispLen 0.10, spread 0.10. Teaching W = (0.40, 0.24) at time 2 s has sd = 0.05, mask 0, and stores white. Lesson 9.13 would store black at that pixel.

The live course checkpoint is Lesson 9.12. Lesson 9.14 is the pedagogical end of Chapter 9, and it is generated, not confirmed. Confirming a generated lesson is a later learner decision. This hub does not make that decision.
