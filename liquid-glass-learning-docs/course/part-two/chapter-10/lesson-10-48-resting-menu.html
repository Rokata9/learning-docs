Lesson 10.48 — Build: the resting menu’s outline, rim line and shadow

Build one Metal function that colors this menu. Follow P to see how a rounded outline gives the tint a soft edge, how the lamp brightens the rim line, and how a shifted copy of the outline darkens the page.

Where this is used. Lesson 11.1 prepares a glass picture with the page, tint, and menu rows for the shader to read. The lens build in 11.4 uses that picture where this build uses a flat tint, so nearby stripes or rows bend at the edge of the same resting outline.

What comes together

  • A signed distance says how far a pixel is from the outline. A soft mask turns that distance into a share from 0 to 1. Rounded rectangle · Soft mask
  • outwardAt finds which way is out. addRimLine uses that direction and the light from all around to brighten the tint. Lesson 10.46 · Lesson 10.47
  • The shadow uses the same distance rule with a wider fade and a shifted read. Shifted read

Section 1 · worked buildOne pixel from outline to final color

Positions are in screen points (pt) on a 402 × 874 pt screen. The origin is the top-left corner. x increases to the right and y increases down. A device pixel is a tiny square the screen lights up. We use a 3 px-per-pt screen for the example. The drawings enlarge the screen so you can see the edge. In the arithmetic, green marks known inputs and ≈ marks a rounded result.

    Find P’s distance from the outline

    The menu’s measured center is (238.8, 742.6) pt. Its measured size is 261 × 188 pt and we model each corner as part of a circle of radius 36 pt. These values come from the resting Safari frame and are good to about ±4 pt. The shape code takes half the width and half the height so it can count out from the center.

    Half width = 261 ÷ 2 = 130.5 pt
    Half height = 188 ÷ 2 = 94 pt
    Left edge x = 238.8 − 130.5 = 108.3 pt
    Top edge y = 742.6 − 94 = 648.6 pt

    K is the center of the upper-left corner circle. Start at the left and top edges and move 36 pt inward along each axis.

    K.x = 108.3 + 36 = 144.3 pt
    K.y = 648.6 + 36 = 684.6 pt

    We choose P at (122.76, 655.88) pt so the next step lands in the soft edge. The straight distance from K to P comes from the two legs of the right triangle. The outline lies at the circle’s radius. Subtract that radius from the length to get signed distance (sd). A minus means inside, zero means on the outline, and a plus means outside.

    Give the tint a soft edge

    At the outline, give half the pixel to the glass. As P moves one full pixel farther out, the formula would lose one whole share. Divide signed distance by the width of one pixel in points to get that change in share. clamp keeps the result between 0 (no glass) and 1 (all glass).

    Pixel width = 1 px ÷ 3 px per pt = ⅓ pt
    Distance from outline in pixel widths = −0.10 ÷ (⅓) = −0.30
    Glass share = 0.50 − (−0.30) = 0.80
    Coverage = clamp(0.80, 0, 1) = 0.80

    The mask is a practical estimate of the share covered by glass. It fades from 1 at half a pixel inside to 0 at half a pixel outside. Use one pixel at the device’s actual scale so the fade stays one physical pixel wide.

    For the light build, the tint is a chosen flat gray of 0.94 per channel on a 0 to 1 scale. That leaves only a little room for the bright line so we can see a channel reach 1. Equal red, green and blue give gray. The dark build uses 0.10, close to the measured interior gray of 25 out of 255. A larger tint channel makes the menu body lighter.

    Find which way the edge faces

    outwardAt takes P and the menu shape. It reads signed distance half a point each way along x and y. The positions within each pair are 1.00 pt apart because 0.50 + 0.50 = 1.00. Each sample uses the same distance from K minus the radius that we used at P.

    Subtract left from right and top from bottom, then divide each change by the full gap. These changes form an arrow toward increasing signed distance, which is outward. Divide both parts by the arrow’s length to keep only its direction. If both changes are zero, the helper returns a zero arrow.

    Add the lit rim line to the tint

    addRimLine takes the tint, P’s signed distance, and that outward direction. The line builds from zero 1.20 pt inside to full weight at the outline. This width fits the roughly 1 pt line in the recording. smoothstep rounds off both ends of the fade. It multiplies the fraction squared by a curve factor. The factor is 3 minus twice the fraction. The helper skips the line when signed distance is positive.

    The outward direction estimates how the glass faces at the outline near P. That arrow lies flat on the screen, with height part 0. The fitted lamp arrow (−0.48, −0.60, 0.64) points left, up the screen, and out toward you. Multiply matching parts and add them to get the alignment. Use its size so the opposite corner can light up too.

    The chosen all-around light gives every part of the line 0.40. On this scale, 0 means no light and 1 means full light. The 0.40 keeps the dim corners visible. That leaves 1.00 − 0.40 = 0.60 for the lamp. The fitted strength 0.125 is the amount of white at full band and full light. Multiply by the band to set how much line reaches P, and by the light to set how bright it is there. Add the result to every tint channel. A channel above 1 is shown as 1.

    Shade the page underneath P

    To draw the shadow 4 pt lower, ask how much mask there was 4 pt above P. The arrow starts at the pixel we color and ends at source S. Positive y goes down, so the read subtracts the offset. A larger offset moves the shadow farther below the menu.

    S.x = P.x = 122.76 pt
    S.y = 655.88 − 4 = 651.88 pt

    Use the same distance rule at S. Now divide by the shadow’s 12 pt fade width. The mask fades from full at 6 pt inside to zero at 6 pt outside. A larger width spreads the transition over more screen points.

    Multiply the mask by the maximum shadow amount. We choose 0.24 so the shadow is easy to see on a flat backing. A larger amount makes the shadow darker. The 12 pt width and 4 pt offset are course starting values. The shadow is indistinguishable in the supplied dark recording. The light tint and backing here are choices for the exercise.

    The light build starts with a page gray of 0.80 per channel. The dark build chooses 0.16. A black shadow takes away the shadow share of the page, so multiply the page color by the share that survives.

    Combine the two shares at P

    The lit tint is the glass color. Give it P’s 0.80 coverage from Step 2. The rest of P shows the shaded page from Step 5. Use the clamped glass color so the glass share contributes at most white. Multiply each color by its share, then add the two contributions. The caller returns alpha 1 because the result already includes the opaque page.

    At full coverage, the page contributes zero. Beyond the soft edge, the glass contributes zero and the shaded page can still show the shadow. In the full view, look for two brighter opposite corners and the wider shadow that extends below the menu.

    Pixel R is at (240, 840.6) pt. The straight bottom edge is at y = 836.6 pt. The shadow moves 4 pt down, and the shadow mask is 0.50 on the outline. Find the read position that supplies R’s shadow mask.

    Source x = 240 pt
    Source y = 840.6 − 4 = 836.6 pt
    Shadow signed distance = 836.6 − 836.6 = 0 pt
    Shadow mask = 0.50

    The read goes up to the original outline. The shadow at R gets half its maximum amount. The tint coverage at R is zero because R is 4 pt outside the menu, past the half-pixel fade.

    Answer key and the function line by line

    half3 holds red, green and blue in the GPU’s short number format. The shape and masks use float for position and distance arithmetic.

    half3 restingMenuColor(float2 P, float pixelPt, half3 tint, half3 page,
                        float shadowOffsetPt, float shadowStrength) {
    float4 box = float4(238.8, 742.6, 130.5, 94.0);
    float radius = 36.0;
    float sd = roundedRectSd(P - box.xy, box.zw, radius);
    float cover = clamp(0.5 - sd / pixelPt, 0.0, 1.0);

    float2 source = P - float2(0.0, shadowOffsetPt);
    float shadowSd = roundedRectSd(source - box.xy, box.zw, radius);
    float shadowMask = clamp(0.5 - shadowSd / 12.0, 0.0, 1.0);
    float shadowAmount = shadowStrength * shadowMask;
    float3 shadedPage = float3(page) * (1.0 - shadowAmount);

    float2 outward = outwardAt(P, box, radius);
    half3 lit = addRimLine(tint, sd, outward);
    float3 glass = clamp(float3(lit), 0.0, 1.0);

    return half3(glass * cover + shadedPage * (1.0 - cover));
}
