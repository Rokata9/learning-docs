Lesson 10.47 — The rim line: a thin band on the outline, lit from both sides

Follows the system’s light or dark setting · desktop · works from file://.

Why: Safari's menu has a thin bright line on its outline: the rim line. In the recording it is brightest in two opposite corners, top-left and bottom-right. A shader draws it from two numbers at each pixel. The band says how much of the line the pixel gets: 1 on the outline, 0 from 1.20 pt inside. The light says how bright the line is at that place. A lamp is at the top left: the line is lit where the glass faces the lamp, and also where it faces away. At P, in the top-left corner: band 0.784 and light 0.8608. With the line's strength, 0.125, each color channel gets 0.0843584 more.

Where this is used. Lesson 10.48 writes this line, the menu's outline and its shadow as one Metal function. Lesson 14.3 lets the line follow the outline while the menu opens.

Uses

  • Lesson 10.46: outward at a pixel, the arrow 1 long that points straight out of the glass, from sd at four neighbors.
  • Lesson 8.11, Lesson 9.3: smoothstep(a, b, x): 0 up to a, 1 from b on, a smooth rise between. Here x is sd.
  • Lesson 10.1: the edge of the glass, seen from the side, is a quarter of a circle. Lesson 10.3: N, the arrow 1 long at 90° to the surface.
  • Lesson 10.38, Lesson 10.42: dot(N, L), the three products of the parts, added: how much a surface faces the light.
  • Lesson 10.43: a surface that faces away from the lamp gets max(dot, 0) = 0. Lesson 10.45: light from all around, plus the lamp's share.
  • Lesson 9.4: abs(x), the number x without its sign.
  • Lesson 3.10: adding one color to another; a channel holds at most 1, so a sum above 1 is shown as 1.

In a calculation, green marks a number known before the calculation starts: given, or found earlier. The other numbers are worked out in it. Pictures of the screen are dark in both themes: the recording is in dark mode.
