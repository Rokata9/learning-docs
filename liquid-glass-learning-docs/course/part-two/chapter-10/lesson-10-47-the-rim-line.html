Lesson 10.47 — The rim line

You’ll work out the color at P. First find how much of the line reaches P, then use the direction the glass faces to find how brightly the line is lit there.

Where this is used. Lesson 10.48 calls addRimLine on the tinted menu color. The thin line appears on top of the tint, with two opposite corners brighter than the others.

Uses

  • Distance to the outline can set the weight of a soft fade. Lesson 9.3.
  • Nearby distances give an arrow pointing straight out on the screen. Lesson 10.46.
  • An arrow at a right angle to the surface shows which way it faces. Lesson 10.3.
  • Two arrows give a score for how well they line up. Lesson 10.42.
  • The lamp can add to the light already coming from all around. Lesson 10.45.
  • Adding the same amount to red, green, and blue adds white. Lesson 3.10.

Screen positions use screen points (pt) from the top left. The x direction goes right and y goes down. The drawings enlarge these distances. In the calculations, green marks a known input and ≈ marks a rounded result.
