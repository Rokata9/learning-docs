What “squaring” means here, and where x⁴ comes from

Squaring an equation means multiplying each side by itself. It removes a square root, because √A × √A = A. When x is outside the root and also inside it, the square of the outside x (x²) meets the x² from inside the root, and x² × x² = x⁴. Two small examples first; then our condition, line by line.
